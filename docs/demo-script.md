# Sift demo script (2 minutes)

Record the dashboard at `/dashboard` on the left and the owner's iMessage thread with Sift on the right. The caller dials the Photon number. Sift's ElevenLabs agent answers on that line.

If nobody can place the live call, use the dashboard Demo control for the picture.

Say the voiceover. The teammates say only the quoted lines.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

**Voiceover:** "People reported losing $3.5 billion to imposter scams in 2025. **VERIFY BEFORE USE.** Source to check: Federal Trade Commission press release, June 15, 2026, https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025. Sift answers your phone. Noise goes in. Only the real calls come through."

**On screen:** Dashboard is quiet. Badge says Waiting. Risk gauge is at 0. The iMessage thread is visible and idle.

## 0:15–1:05 — Tax scam

The scammer teammate calls the Photon number.

**Sift (already the first message):** "Hi, this is an AI assistant for Alex Kim. This call may be recorded. Who are you trying to reach?"

**Scammer:**

1. "Hello. This is Dale Pruitt with the Northline Revenue Service. I need Alex Kim on the line about an unpaid federal tax file."
2. "Alex owes four thousand eight hundred dollars. A case was opened this morning, and I have to settle it before this call ends."
3. "If this is not paid within the hour, a warrant goes out and officers come to the home. Do not hang up, and do not tell anyone about this case."
4. "The only way to stop the arrest is to buy eight Northwind gift cards for five hundred dollars each and read me the codes right now. Stay on the line while you drive to the store."

**On screen:** The live transcript fills in. Name check flips to match. The gauge climbs. By the gift-card line it is past 75 and the badge turns Blocked. Reason chips mention the tax claim, the arrest threat, and the gift cards.

**Owner's phone:** an iMessage arrives during the call, in the form "Possible tax scam" with the score, and a Block / Allow poll.

**Owner, on camera, taps Block or replies:** `BLOCK`

**Voiceover:** "The owner never picks up. One reply blocks the number and ends the call."

**On screen:** The call ends. The badge stays Blocked.

## 1:05–1:45 — Doctor's office

The doctor's-office teammate calls the same Photon number.

**Sift:** "Hi, this is an AI assistant for Alex Kim. This call may be recorded. Who are you trying to reach?"

**Doctor's office:**

1. "Hi, this is Priya at Dr. Okonkwo's office. I'm calling for Alex Kim about Thursday's checkup."
2. "Dr. Okonkwo has hospital rounds on Thursday, so we need to move the visit. Nothing is wrong with the results, and there is no bill to pay on this call."
3. "Friday at 2pm or Monday at 10am are both open at the same clinic. No new paperwork, and Alex can reach us on the main line, 206-555-0148."
4. "Yes, please book Friday at 2pm. Alex should come to the same office. That is the only change."

**On screen:** The gauge stays under 40. Name check matches. The badge moves from Screening to Forwarding.

**Owner's phone rings.** The owner hears a short whisper: the call is Dr. Okonkwo's office about moving Thursday's checkup. Then the owner is in the call with Priya.

**Voiceover:** "Same number. This one is real, so Sift rings you, tells you why, and puts you on."

## 1:45–2:00 — Close

**Voiceover:** "After each call, the summary lands in this iMessage thread. You can answer CALL, BLOCK, or REPORT. Sift is the sieve. Scams stop. The doctor's office gets through."

**On screen:** The iMessage summary for the doctor's call is on the phone. The dashboard call strip shows the blocked tax call and the forwarded clinic call.
